# CMP-003 — Jurisdiction Identification

## Canonical Definition
Identifies geographic, sovereign, and legal jurisdictions that may impose obligations on the organization or its activities.

## Purpose
Establish the universe of potentially relevant legal regimes before applicability is decided.

## Boundaries
Identifies potentially relevant jurisdictions; CMP-014 determines whether a specific obligation actually applies.
