# CMP-001 — Compliance Context Definition

## Canonical Definition
Defines the internal and external conditions that determine how an organization identifies, interprets, implements, evaluates, and demonstrates conformity with obligations.

## Purpose
Establish a defensible compliance environment before individual requirements are analyzed.

## Boundaries
Does not determine individual requirement applicability or conformity; it establishes the environment in which those determinations occur. Governance establishes authority and policy; Risk analyzes uncertainty arising from the environment.
