# GOV-032 — Change Governance

## Canonical metadata
**Domain:** Governance  
**Class / family:** Adaptation  
**Status:** Seed  
**Evidence confidence:** Exploratory

## Canonical Definition
Governs organizational, technical, policy, regulatory, process, and emergency changes through authority, impact assessment, approval, communication, verification, and rollback rules.

## Purpose
Allows controlled adaptation while limiting unintended consequences and preserving accountability.

## Scope Boundary
Governs authorization and oversight of change; execution of individual changes occurs in operational functions.
