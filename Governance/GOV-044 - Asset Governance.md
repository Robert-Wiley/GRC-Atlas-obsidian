# GOV-044 — Asset Governance

## Canonical metadata
**Domain:** Governance  
**Class / family:** Accountability  
**Status:** Seed  
**Evidence confidence:** Exploratory

## Canonical Definition
Establishes ownership, classification, custodianship, lifecycle, protection, criticality, utilization, acquisition, and disposal expectations for tangible and intangible assets.

## Purpose
Ensures organizational assets remain identifiable, accountable, protected, and aligned with enterprise value and obligations.

## Scope Boundary
Governs asset responsibilities and lifecycle expectations; physical or technical asset management is operational execution.
