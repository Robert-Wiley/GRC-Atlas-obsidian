# GOV-051 — Governance Signal and Decision Integrity

## Canonical metadata
**Domain:** Governance  
**Class / family:** Measurement  
**Status:** Seed

## Canonical Definition
Governs the integrity, timing, fidelity, confidence, escalation, interpretation, and decision usefulness of signals flowing through organizational governance systems.

## Purpose
Determines whether governing authorities receive a sufficiently accurate representation of organizational reality to make sound decisions and whether those decisions propagate as intended.

## Scope Boundary
Focuses on signal integrity and decision-state correspondence across governance layers; source-domain analytics remain with their originating functions.
