# GOV-001 — Organizational Purpose, Direction, and Identity

## Scope
Includes mission, vision, values, strategic intent, organizational identity, governing principles, stakeholder promises, long-term outcomes, and the legitimacy claims that explain why the organization exists.

## Boundaries
Does not include execution of strategy, operational planning, or performance delivery. Those functions consume this direction; they do not define it.

## Core Mechanisms
- Mission and vision statements
- Enterprise values and ethical principles
- Strategic intent and governing principles
- Stakeholder commitments and public-interest obligations
- Purpose reviews and strategic refresh cycles

## Primary Actors
Board, chief executive, executive leadership, founders/owners, strategy leaders, ethics/legal advisers, and key stakeholder representatives.
