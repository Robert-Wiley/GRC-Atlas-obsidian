# GOV-036 — Issue and Exception Governance

## Canonical metadata
**Domain:** Governance  
**Class / family:** Decision  
**Status:** Seed  
**Evidence confidence:** Exploratory

## Canonical Definition
Governs identification, ownership, prioritization, approval, expiration, monitoring, remediation, escalation, and closure of issues, waivers, exceptions, and accepted deviations.

## Purpose
Allows controlled deviation from expected states while preserving visibility, accountability, and residual exposure ownership.

## Scope Boundary
Governance authorizes and oversees deviations; risk evaluates exposure and compliance determines whether exceptions are permissible.
