## Canonical Definition
Establishes the authorities, appetite, tolerance, ownership, escalation, oversight, reporting, and decision architecture through which risk is governed.

## Purpose
Defines how uncertainty and exposure enter organizational decisions without performing the underlying risk analysis itself.

## Scope Boundary
Governance sets risk boundaries and decision rights; identification, analysis, evaluation, quantification, and treatment belong primarily to Risk.
